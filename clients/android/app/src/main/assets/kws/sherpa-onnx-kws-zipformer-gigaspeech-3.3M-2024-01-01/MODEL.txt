English zipformer keyword spotter, about 3.3M parameters, trained on GigaSpeech XL.
The int8 encoder, int8 decoder, and int8 joiner are the ones bundled here.
tokens.txt and bpe.model (sentencepiece unigram, 500 pieces) encode a wake phrase
into the keyword line this model scores. The model is not downloaded at runtime.
